\section*{Bug 24: solveset returns 1 for asec(x) = 2*pi outside the principal asec range}

\begin{counterexamplebox}
\textbf{Task.} Solve the inverse-secant equation over the complex domain:
\[
  \operatorname{asec}(x) = 2\pi .
\]

\medskip
\textbf{SymPy's answer.}
\[
  \operatorname{solveset}(\operatorname{asec}(x)=2\pi, x, \mathbb{C})
  = \{1\}.
\]

\medskip
\textbf{Correct answer.}
\[
  \varnothing .
\]

\medskip
\textbf{Explanation.} Substitution rejects the returned value:
\(\operatorname{asec}(1)-2\pi=-2\pi\).  Principal inverse secant satisfies
\(\operatorname{asec}(1)=\arccos(1)=0\), so applying \(\sec\) to both sides has
introduced an extraneous solution.

\medskip
\textbf{Likely root cause.} The diagnosis identifies
\nolinkurl{sympy/solvers/solveset.py}'s generic \texttt{.inverse()} path in
\texttt{\_invert\_complex}: \(\operatorname{asec}^{-1}\) is treated as \(\sec\)
without a principal-range check or final candidate verification.  This is a new
instance of a known failure mode.

\medskip
\textbf{Artifact (GitHub).} \bugbundle{bug-024-solveset-returns-1-for-asec-x-2-pi-outside-the-principal-ase}
\end{counterexamplebox}
