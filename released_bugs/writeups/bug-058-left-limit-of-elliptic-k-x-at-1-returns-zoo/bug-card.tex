\section*{Bug 58: Left Limit of elliptic\_k(x) at 1 Returns zoo}

\begin{counterexamplebox}
\textbf{Task.} Evaluate the real left-hand limit of the complete elliptic integral:
\[
\lim_{x\to1^-}\operatorname{elliptic\_k}(x).
\]

\medskip
\textbf{SymPy's answer.}
\[
\operatorname{limit}(\operatorname{elliptic\_k}(x),x,1,\mathrm{dir}=-)=\mathrm{zoo}.
\]

\medskip
\textbf{Correct answer.}
\[
\lim_{x\to1^-}\operatorname{elliptic\_k}(x)=+\infty.
\]

\medskip
\textbf{Explanation.} For real \(m\to1^-\), \(K(m)\) has leading behavior
\[
K(m)=-\frac12\log(1-m)+O(1),
\]
so it diverges through positive real values. Returning \(\mathrm{zoo}\) loses the one-sided real sign and is not equivalent to \(+\infty\).

\medskip
\textbf{Likely root cause.} The diagnosis traces the limit through \texttt{elliptic\_k.\_eval\_nseries}, which delegates the expansion near \(m=1\) to a hypergeometric rewrite that returns \texttt{nan} for \(\operatorname{elliptic\_k}(1-w)\). The missing branch-aware logarithmic expansion makes the limit machinery fall back to unsigned complex infinity; this is a new instance of a known failure mode.

\medskip

\end{counterexamplebox}
