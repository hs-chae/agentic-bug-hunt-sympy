\section*{Bug 30: solveset over the reals treats a principal fractional-power identity as true for negative inputs}

\begin{counterexamplebox}
\textbf{Task.} Solve the principal fractional-power equation over the real domain.
\[
  (x^2)^{1/3}=x^{2/3}
\]

\medskip
\textbf{SymPy's answer.}
\[
  \operatorname{solveset}\!\left((x^2)^{1/3}=x^{2/3},\ x,\ \mathbb{R}\right)=\mathbb{R}.
\]

\medskip
\textbf{Correct answer.}
\[
  [0,\infty)
\]

\medskip
\textbf{Explanation.} Substituting \(x=-1\), the left side is \(((-1)^2)^{1/3}=1\), while the right side is the principal complex value \((-1)^{2/3}=\exp(2\pi i/3)\).  Thus a result containing all negative real numbers includes false solutions.

\medskip
\textbf{Likely root cause.} The diagnosis traces the call through \texttt{unrad}, which cubes both radical terms and collapses the equation to \(x^2=x^2\), and then through \texttt{\_solve\_radical}, which accepts the resulting whole-domain real solution without restoring the lost branch condition.  This is a new instance of a known failure mode.

\medskip
\textbf{Artifact (GitHub).} \bugbundle{bug-030-solveset-over-the-reals-treats-a-principal-fractional-power-}
\end{counterexamplebox}
